MaybeGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short. MaybeGo keeps one shared list of "maybe" events for the parents of one family. We keep only what that list needs. Flyer photos you capture are read by Anthropic's Claude AI to fill in the event details, and your photos are never used to train AI. We have no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and data inside the app at any time.

Who we are

MaybeGo is an iPhone app made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: skeyex.ai@gmail.com.

We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar contents, browsing activity or advertising identifiers. MaybeGo only adds an event to your calendar when you ask it to; it never reads your calendar.

Who receives your data

  • Your family. Everything on the shared list, including flyer photos, is visible to the other members of your family in MaybeGo, and to nobody else.
  • Supabase stores the account, the list and the photos for us, on servers in the United States (Amazon Web Services, North Virginia). Access rules let only your family's members read your family's data.
  • Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI) receives each flyer photo once, to read the event details. Before your first capture the app asks for your permission, names Anthropic, and sends nothing until you agree. Without agreeing you can still type maybes in.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, and delivers notifications to your iPhone.
  • Expo (a push service) passes notifications on to Apple. A notification holds only your phone's push token, its words (titles of your family's maybes and, when your partner adds a maybe, your partner's name in MaybeGo), and a random code that tells the app which maybe to open.

These services handle data only to provide their part of MaybeGo, under their own terms and privacy commitments, which give the same or equal protection as this policy. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI and your photos

  • Each photo is sent to Anthropic's API only to read the event details on it.
  • We never allow your photos to be used to train AI. Anthropic says it does not train its models on what apps send it without that app's express permission. MaybeGo has not given that permission and never will.
  • Anthropic says it deletes what apps send it within 30 days. It may keep content its safety systems flag for up to 2 years, or where the law requires. See Anthropic's page on data retention.

How long we keep it

  • Past maybes move to Past the day after the event and are deleted, with their photos, 30 days later.
  • Removed maybes ("Not this time") are deleted for good a day after you remove them.
  • Photos are deleted with the last maybe that uses them; a photo no maybe uses is deleted after two days.
  • Your account and family are kept until you delete your account.
  • Server logs hold only ids, counts and error codes, never a photo, the AI's answer, an invite code or a token.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account at once and cancels Sign in with Apple for MaybeGo. You see a confirmation when it is done.

  • If you are the last member of your family, the family, all its maybes and all its photos are deleted too.
  • If your partner is still a member, the shared list stays for them. This includes the maybes and photos you added: they stay on the list, no longer marked as added by you. Your own account, name and settings are deleted.
  • If your Apple account itself is deleted, Apple tells us and we delete your MaybeGo data in the same way.

You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing skeyex.ai@gmail.com.

Turning off Sign in with Apple

If you stop using Sign in with Apple for MaybeGo (in your iPhone's Settings), MaybeGo signs you out on every phone and removes your notification tokens. It does not delete your account or your family's list. Signing in again with the same Apple ID brings them back. To delete your data, use Delete account (above).

Your choices

  • Notifications and camera access are optional and can be turned off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • You can edit or remove any maybe at any time; changes show for your partner too.
  • You can change your name and settings in the app.
  • To withdraw your agreement to the AI notice, or for a copy of your data, email us.

Data kept only on your phone

To work offline and to remember your last view, MaybeGo keeps a copy of your list and a few small settings on your phone, and your sign-in in the iPhone's Keychain. These are removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Children

MaybeGo is meant for parents and other adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access rules on the server limit every row and photo to its family's members. Secret keys are kept only on the server, never in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app first. We will never start using your photos to train AI.
